## 1. Intro  (0:00)

Hello! This is your very own iPod.

It plays your music and your audiobooks, and it keeps them on the iPod, so they play anywhere, even with no Wi-Fi.

In this video I will show you how everything works. You can watch it all the way through, or pick a part from the Help list at the bottom of the menu, any time you like.


## 2. Finding your way around  (0:30)

Turn the wheel to move up and down the list. Press the middle button to choose.

The Menu button takes you back.

If you leave the iPod alone for a minute, the buttons go to sleep so nothing happens in your pocket. To wake them, press the same button three times, quickly.


## 3. Music  (0:56)

Choose Music to find your songs. You can look by artist or by kind of music, and New songs shows what has just arrived.

Pick an artist, and you will see a row of their albums. Pick an album to see its songs. Choose All songs to see everything by that artist.

You can shuffle too. Shuffle all music plays everything in a surprise order. Open an artist's All songs, or an album, and choose Shuffle to mix up just those.

While a song plays, the words scroll along in time, if the song has them.


## 4. Favourites  (1:40)

When you hear a song you love, hold down the middle button on it. A little heart appears.

Your hearts all live together in Favourites, near the top of Music. Hold the middle button again to take a heart away.


## 5. Audiobooks  (1:59)

Your audiobooks are in Audiobooks. Choose one to start listening.

The iPod remembers where you stopped, even if you switch it off, and it will carry on from the same place. If a book has chapters, you can choose one from the Chapters list.


## 6. Searching  (2:20)

To find something new, choose Search. Turn the wheel to find a letter, and press the middle button to add it. When you have spelled it, press Play.

Or do it the easy way. Hold the middle button anywhere except on a song, and say what you want, like, give you better. Let go, and the iPod finds it.

Songs already on your iPod come first. Then, if you have Wi-Fi, you will see more songs to stream. Choose one to play it, or add it to your library so it stays on your iPod for ever.


## 7. Listening on speakers and headphones  (3:04)

Choose Listen on to play your music on a speaker in the house, like the one in your room. It carries on from the same place.

For headphones, go to Settings and choose Bluetooth headphones. Put your headphones in pairing mode, choose Search for headphones, and pick them from the list. Turn the volume with the wheel or the buttons on the headphones.


## 8. Bedtime  (3:32)

Every night there is a bedtime window, and a little time to listen. When your time is used up, the iPod fades out and says goodnight.

That is not a punishment. It is to help you sleep. If a grown-up says it is fine, they can unlock it for a while with their special code. They can see and change your bedtime from their phone, too.


## 9. Finding your iPod  (4:02)

If you ever lose your iPod, a grown-up can make it ring from their phone, loud, for a whole minute. Press any button to stop it.

The lock screen also shows who to call, so anyone who finds it can get it back to you. A grown-up sets that in Home Assistant.


## 10. Settings  (4:26)

In Settings you can join Wi-Fi, change how bright the screen is, and choose how the wheel clicks.

Some settings are for grown-ups, and ask for the code. Joining Wi-Fi is fine for anyone, but taking a network away needs a grown-up. Grown-ups can also change many of these from their phone.


## 11. That is everything  (4:50)

That is everything! Your music, your books, your favourites, and your voice.

You can watch any part again from the Help list. Now go and enjoy your iPod.
