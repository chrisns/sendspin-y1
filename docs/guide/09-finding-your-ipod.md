# 9. Finding your iPod

*If it gets lost*

## Narration

> If you ever lose your iPod, a grown-up can make it ring from their phone, loud, for a whole minute. Press any button to stop it.
>
> The lock screen also shows who to call, so anyone who finds it can get it back to you.

## For grown-ups

*Find it* is a button on the device page in Home Assistant; the player checks in every 30 seconds on Wi-Fi and rings on the alarm stream at full volume. The lock screen shows the owner's "if found" contact lines (a setting in Home Assistant). The Find My beacon (Settings, Home Assistant switch) lets passing iPhones report where it is.
