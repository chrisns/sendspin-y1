# User guide and video script

Each chapter is three things at once: the **narration** is the spoken script for the Help video on the player, the **on screen** list is the shot list (screenshots are generated by `docs/screenshots/capture.py`), and **for grown-ups** is plain documentation.

Chapter order is the order of the video. Run `python3 docs/guide/build.py` after changing a chapter: it checks every screenshot exists and rewrites `SCRIPT.md` (the whole narration in one file, with timings estimated at 2.5 words a second) and `chapters.json`.

1. [Intro](01-intro.md): Welcome: what the iPod is and how it works
2. [Finding your way around](02-finding-your-way.md): The wheel, the buttons, the lock
3. [Music](03-music.md): Your songs, and how to play them
4. [Favourites](04-favourites.md): Keep the songs you love close
5. [Audiobooks](05-audiobooks.md): Pick up exactly where you stopped
6. [Searching](06-searching.md): Type a name, or just say it
7. [Listening on speakers and headphones](07-listening-on.md): Play it in the kitchen, or in your ears
8. [Bedtime](08-bedtime.md): Sleep well
9. [Finding your iPod](09-finding-your-ipod.md): If it gets lost
10. [Settings](10-settings.md): Make it yours
11. [That is everything](11-the-end.md): Have fun
